A single room of glass, lifted five feet above a meadow and a hundred feet from the Fox River, so that the floods pass underneath and the seasons fill every wall.

Facts: Wikipedia · National Trust for Historic Preservation
01 · The approach

Floating above the meadow

Eight white steel columns hold the floor and roof clear of the ground. A travertine terrace steps up to the porch, and the house seems to hover over the grass.

02 · The structure

Nothing but the frame

The columns are welded to the outside of the slabs, so no structure interrupts the glass. Every elevation is a window onto the trees.

03 · The river

Built for the flood

The Fox River runs about 100 feet to the south. The raised floor was Mies’s answer to its seasonal floods.
