FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need my own domain or email setup?

No. We provide pre-warmed sending domains connected to your brand. If you’d rather send from your own inboxes, you can connect them in a couple of clicks.

Can I review emails before they go out?

Yes. You can approve every email, approve the first batch only, or let it run fully on autopilot once you’re happy with the tone.

Where do the contacts come from?

We combine public company data with verified business contact sources, then check every address before sending.

Is cold email allowed?

B2B outreach is legal in most countries when it’s relevant, identifies the sender and offers an easy opt-out. Every email includes one, and opt-outs are honoured automatically.

How fast will I see replies?

Most campaigns start sending the same day. Replies usually start coming in within the first week, depending on your market and offer.

Does it cost anything?

No. Shake Outreach is our own in-house tool, built for our team. There are no plans or fees.
