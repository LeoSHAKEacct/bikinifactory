    Rent it short-term

    Hand the suite to Be Happy, the building's exclusive operator. They list it, take bookings, clean it, maintain it and pay you your share.

    Use it yourself

    Keep it as your own place by the sea in Cartagena, furnished and ready from day one.

    Mix the two

    Block the weeks you want to be there and let it earn for the rest of the year. AIRA is designed so each owner chooses.

    The operation

    One operator. Nothing for you to run.

    Be Happy is a specialist in managing real estate assets and the only operator authorised in AIRA. You get the earnings without the work of running a rental.

    • Booking system and channel management
    • Guest supplies, cleaning and laundry
    • Maintenance of suites, building and common areas
    • Security, surveillance and insurance
    • Licences, permits and tourism fund
    • Accounting, external audit and taxes
    • Marketing, photography and listings
    • Customer service around the clock
