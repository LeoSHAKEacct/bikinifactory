The Developer Tier List

Not all developers are equal.

"We always hear on social media…" that every project is a great deal. Hasan's framework cuts through that by ranking developers before recommending a single unit.

A
Proven & institutional

Long delivery track record, strong resale liquidity, and handover quality that holds value through the cycle.

B
Established & reliable

Solid history with competitive pricing. Often the sweet spot for investors who want yield.

C
Emerging, with conditions

Can work in the right location and at the right price, with extra checks on escrow and delivery timelines.

D
Proceed with caution

Limited track record or delays. Most of the time the right call is to walk away.
