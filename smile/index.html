Treatments

What we do for your smile

Every treatment starts with a consultation. We explain the plan, the timing and how many days you'll need in Medellín.

01

Crowns & bridges

A crown covers a natural tooth to bring back its shape, strength, size and look, made to measure for each patient. A bridge replaces missing teeth by resting on the teeth beside them.

  • Made to measure for your mouth
  • Matched to the colour of your teeth
  • A single tooth or a full span

02

Dental implants

Implants made to bring back function, looks and confidence in your smile. A titanium post replaces the root, and a crown sits on top that looks and feels like a natural tooth.

  • Replaces the whole tooth, root included
  • Leaves the neighbouring teeth alone
  • Planned in stages around your trip

03

InvisiSmile

Clear aligners. Based on what your teeth need, our dentist designs a detailed treatment plan to move them little by little, without anyone really noticing.

  • Clear and removable
  • A plan designed for you
  • No brackets, no wires

04

Dental cleaning

To keep your smile clean, fresh and free of problems. We remove the tartar and plaque your brush can't reach and check that everything is in order.

  • Tartar and plaque removed
  • A full check-up
  • The best start for any treatment

Whitening

A few shades lighter, without harming the enamel.

Fillings & inlays

We swap dark metal fillings for tooth-coloured material.
